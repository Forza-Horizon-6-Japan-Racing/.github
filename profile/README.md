# Forza Horizon 6 — Japan Open-World Racing Adventure

---

## What is Forza Horizon 6?

Forza Horizon 6 is an open-world racing game developed by Playground Games and set in Japan. It brings the Horizon Festival into a landscape of mountain passes, coastal roads, rural villages, and the bustling streets of Tokyo. Players begin their journey as visitors to Japan and work their way into the festival, taking part in races, discovering new locations, collecting cars, and building a reputation through a variety of driving challenges.

The game combines competitive racing with the freedom to travel across a detailed world at an individual pace. Fast road cars, classic Japanese vehicles, off-road machines, and other automotive favourites offer different driving experiences across changing terrain. With more than 550 cars, extensive customization, and a broad range of events, the game gives players many ways to develop their collection and choose how they approach the festival.

Japan is central to the experience, with its distinct automotive culture, mountain roads, city districts, and scenic countryside. Tokyo provides a large urban environment with different neighbourhoods and industrial areas, while winding mountain routes encourage precise driving and vehicle control. Exploration also reveals collectible cars, landmarks, houses, and other discoveries that contribute to the player's journey.

Beyond racing, Forza Horizon 6 includes vehicle tuning, customizable garages, player-created events, and shared activities with friends. Players can build their collection, adjust cars for different driving conditions, decorate personal spaces, and participate in festival competitions. These systems combine open-world exploration with a flexible racing experience that rewards both driving skill and curiosity.

## 🏁 Horizon Festival & Racing Career

The Horizon Festival provides the main structure for the racing journey. Players arrive in Japan as visitors and work toward joining the festival by completing qualifying events. As they earn new wristbands and rise through the ranks, additional races, showcases, and challenges become available.

The campaign combines structured objectives with the freedom to discover events across the map. Players can concentrate on festival competitions or spend time driving through the countryside, finding collectibles, and visiting new locations. Special showcase events introduce spectacular driving challenges, while Horizon Rush courses test precision across obstacle-filled routes.

Earning the Gold Wristband unlocks Legend Island, a dedicated region with its own events and areas to discover. This gives the festival journey a clear long-term goal while leaving room for exploration between major competitions.

## 🚗 Car Collection & Automotive Culture

The game features more than 550 cars spanning different manufacturers, driving styles, and performance categories. The collection includes Japanese automotive favourites alongside vehicles suited to road racing, drifting, off-road driving, and other event types.

Discoveries are part of the collecting experience. Aftermarket Cars appear around the world and can be tested before being added to a collection. This creates an additional reason to travel through Japan rather than relying exclusively on race rewards and menus.

The garage becomes a personal record of the player's journey. Collecting different vehicles opens new ways to approach events, experiment with driving styles, and build a selection suited to different roads and competition formats.

## 🏎️ Vehicle Tuning & Customization

Customization allows players to adjust the appearance and performance of their cars. Tuning setups can be tailored to different routes and driving conditions, while selected vehicles support body kits and additional styling options. Window liveries and separate front and rear rim choices add further possibilities for personal designs.

Performance adjustments matter when preparing a vehicle for a particular event. A setup suited to winding mountain roads may feel different from one intended for high-speed circuits or rough terrain. Experimenting with handling and other available settings helps players find a balance that matches their driving style.

Cosmetic tire wear and refreshed steering animations also contribute to the driving presentation. Together, these systems connect visual customization with the experience of using a car across the world.

## 🗾 Japan's Roads, Landscapes & Tokyo

Japan offers a varied driving environment that combines dense urban districts with mountain passes, rural scenery, and coastal routes. Tokyo is the largest urban area in the series, featuring downtown streets, suburban neighbourhoods, docks, and industrial districts. Each environment creates different opportunities for racing and exploration.

Mountain roads encourage careful cornering and vehicle control, while city streets offer a different rhythm of traffic-free festival driving and competitive events. Changes in elevation, road surfaces, and surrounding scenery help distinguish individual routes.

Seasonal environments and detailed landscape work contribute to the atmosphere of the world. From busy streets to quieter countryside roads, the map is designed to reward players who take time to discover locations beyond the main race routes.

## 🏆 Street Races, Touge Battles & Special Events

Forza Horizon 6 includes a variety of racing formats suited to different driving preferences. Street races bring competition to urban routes, while Touge Battles focus on winding mountain roads inspired by Japanese driving culture. Time Attack Circuits offer opportunities to refine racing lines and compare performance against the clock.

Drag Meets add another format centred on acceleration and straight-line performance. Festival showcases and Horizon Rush events introduce more unusual challenges, including obstacle courses and dramatic set pieces.

The range of events encourages players to experiment with different cars and driving techniques. Rather than relying on a single vehicle for every activity, players can build a collection that covers several types of competition.

## 🏡 Player Houses, Garages & the Estate

Player Houses offer more than a place to return between races. Each includes a customizable garage where players can arrange vehicles and create a personal showroom. Garage layouts can be decorated and shared, giving the car collection a more individual presentation.

The Estate expands this creative side of the game into the open world. Players can develop a mountain valley by building and decorating within the available environment. Constructed areas become part of the world and can be visited by friends, connecting personal creativity with exploration.

These features give players goals beyond winning races. Collecting cars, designing spaces, and developing a personal environment become additional ways to shape the Horizon experience.

## 🌐 Multiplayer, Car Meets & Shared Activities

Shared driving activities let players meet in the open world, display customized cars, and set out together across Japan. Car Meets create gathering places inspired by Japanese automotive culture, where players can view vehicles, discover designs, and organize group drives.

Convoys allow friends to travel together and take part in racing activities. Shared LINK skills reward coordinated driving, while Drag Meets and Time Attack Circuits introduce additional ways to compete within the world. The Eliminator and Hide & Seek also return as distinct multiplayer modes.

Cross-play and cross-save support help connect supported platforms and preserve player progress across compatible versions. Availability and account requirements depend on the platform and edition being used.

## 🛠️ EventLab & Creative Building

EventLab gives players tools to create custom events, routes, and driving challenges. These tools extend the game beyond its standard race selection, allowing the community to experiment with layouts and imaginative event concepts.

Horizon CoLab introduces collaborative building, enabling friends to create together within Japan. Custom events and shared creations add variety to the world and encourage players to discover experiences made by other members of the community.

The Estate and customizable garages also contribute to the creative side of the game. Together, these features support players who enjoy designing environments and events as much as driving through them.

## 🎵 Sound, Atmosphere & Accessibility

The soundscape combines engine audio, road-surface interactions, environmental recordings, and a soundtrack featuring Japanese artists. Changes in the surrounding environment help distinguish different driving locations, while engine sounds contribute to the identity of individual vehicles.

Updated steering animations support a more immersive cockpit view, with wheel rotation reaching up to 540 degrees. Players using compatible steering equipment can adjust their setup according to their preferences.

Accessibility options include customizable High Contrast mode, Car Proximity Radar, and AutoDrive. These features offer additional ways to read the environment and experience the world, helping players adapt certain aspects of driving to their needs.
